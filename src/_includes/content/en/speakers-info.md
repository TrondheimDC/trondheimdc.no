### Travel and accommodation

TDC covers travel and accommodation for speakers who need it. In the speaker registration form you can ask for travel and hotel, travel only, hotel only, or none at all. Accommodation is available for the Sunday and/or Monday night.

Unsure what you asked for? You'll find it on your <a href="https://sessionize.com/tdc-2026/dashboard" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">speaker dashboard on Sessionize</a>.

#### Travel

If you have submitted a travel plan, please go ahead and book your tickets as soon as possible — it helps us keep the costs down. If the price has increased drastically since you submitted the form, please let us know and we will do a new evaluation.

**Save your receipts!** A few days after TDC we will send you a form where you can upload your receipts and enter your bank account information, so that we can reimburse your costs.

#### Accommodation

If you have reported a need for accommodation, we will take care of it for the nights you asked for. You'll be staying at the Clarion Hotel Trondheim — the conference venue — so you won't have far to go in the morning.

When you arrive on the reported day, simply check in with your name at reception. There's no booking reference to keep track of.

### Equipment

Each conference room is equipped with a widescreen projector supporting resolutions up to 1920x1080 and aspect ratio 16:9. Connectors for HDMI, DisplayPort, MiniDisplayPort and VGA will be available in each room. If your laptop requires a special video adapter you must remember to bring your own. The rooms have great audio systems for you to use through a 3.5 mm audio jack, and a wired internet connection.

We will also provide additional equipment based on your input from the speaker registration form, but please remember to bring your own power supply, clicker, laser pointer, etc.

**Want to test your setup?** If you wish to verify that your computer/presentation works with the AV-equipment at the venue, that can be arranged Sunday evening after the speaker's dinner.

### Wi-Fi

Wireless internet access is provided throughout the conference hotel, and it has performed very well on previous conferences, even though it is shared among all participants.

### Speaker's Lounge

There will be a speaker's lounge where you can prepare before your talk and unwind after, with refreshments available. It will only be open for speakers and staff.

### Breaks and exhibition partners

During the day we will have eight breaks, including a 1-hour lunch. These give participants time to network and visit our 30+ exhibition partners.

If you're available during breaks, we'd love for you to chat with attendees and answer questions.

### Party

At the end of the conference, a party will be held at the conference hotel — a great way to round off the conference day and get to know fellow attendees in a more informal setting. We hope that all speakers join us at the party. There will be a light meal and a few drink coupons for each attendee.

### Code of Conduct

All participants, speakers, partners, staff, and volunteers at the conference are required to follow the Code of Conduct. TDC strives to be a welcoming and inclusive conference for all attendees, without exception. We do not tolerate physical or digital harassment of our participants or staff in any form.

Not sure who to contact if something happens? All organisers will be wearing t-shirts with CREW printed on the back — please speak up, we can't be everywhere at once.

Read the full [Code of Conduct]({{ '/en/' | url }}#coc).
